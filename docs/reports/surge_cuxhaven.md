# Case report — `surge_cuxhaven`

**Domain** storm_surge · **Target** `residual_m` (m) · **Freq** `1h` · **Horizon** `48h`

*Hourly non-tidal residual (observed sea level minus tide prediction) at the Cuxhaven Steubenhoft GESLA gauge (German Bight). Exogenous: ERA5 mean sea-level pressure + 10-m wind components, fetched at the station point via Open-Meteo (credential-free). Wind and pressure are the physical surge drivers and are listed as known-future: at forecast time they would come from an NWP forecast; here the reanalysis is used as a perfect-forecast proxy. Record trimmed to 2000-2024 so ERA5 forcing covers the whole series.*
