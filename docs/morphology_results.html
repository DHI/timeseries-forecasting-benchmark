Key findings

Finding 1
r = −0.05
Morphological distance does not predict method-ranking agreement. Spearman correlation between morphological distance and rank-vector distance: p = 0.67.
Finding 2
r = +0.57
Spectral entropy is the strongest predictor of overall case difficulty (best achievable MASE). Suggestive at p = 0.055; significant for AutoETS alone (p = 0.033).
Finding 3 — strongest
r = +0.74 **
exog_r2_gain strongly predicts the MASE improvement from adding exogenous variables to LightGBM (p = 0.006). The feature does what it promises.
Bottom line

Morphological similarity is a weak global predictor of shared method rankings — cases that look alike do not systematically prefer the same methods. Two features break through: spectral entropy tracks how hard a case is overall, and exog_r2_gain reliably flags when exogenous-aware methods will outperform their univariate counterparts. The sample (n = 12) is underpowered for most correlations, so the null results should be read as "inconclusive" rather than "no effect."
