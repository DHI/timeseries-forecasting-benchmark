Key findings

Finding 1
ρ = +0.09
Morphological distance does not predict method-ranking agreement. Spearman correlation between morphological distance and rank-vector distance: p = 0.50.
Finding 2
ρ = −0.57
Seasonal strength is the strongest predictor of overall case difficulty (best achievable MASE). Stronger seasonality → easier to forecast. Suggestive at p = 0.051.
Finding 3 — strongest
ρ = +0.74 **
exog_r2_gain strongly predicts the MASE improvement from adding exogenous variables to LightGBM (p = 0.006). The feature does what it promises.
Bottom line

Morphological similarity is a weak global predictor of shared method rankings — cases that look alike do not systematically prefer the same methods. Two features break through: seasonal strength tracks how hard a case is overall (stronger seasonality → lower MASE), and exog_r2_gain reliably flags when exogenous-aware methods will outperform their univariate counterparts. The sample (n = 12) is underpowered for most correlations, so the null results should be read as "inconclusive" rather than "no effect."
